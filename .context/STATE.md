## Known Bugs / Open Items

- **NEW (2026-07-13, presentation-prep session): "For Silversea Media" implication text is repetitive across signals in the same sector — confirmed root cause, not a bug.** `pipeline/analyst.py:247-284`'s `_generate_implications()` is a deliberate, zero-LLM-cost, keyword-matching function (from the 2026-06-29 "Dashboard density overhaul" decision) — it is NOT LLM-generated per signal. It checks each signal's text against ~14 fixed keyword phrases (`SPECIFIC_KEYWORDS`); if none match, it falls back to one of only 6 static per-sector sentences (`SECTOR_IMPLICATIONS`). Since most signals within one sector don't happen to contain one of those 14 exact phrases, they collapse onto the same generic sentence — exactly what Alfonso observed on the VN General tab's Competitors/General News sections. **Deferred, not fixed today** (Alfonso's explicit call — too architecturally/conceptually complex to touch a few hours before presenting, and would require a pipeline re-run afterward). Alfonso's stated future direction: make this section genuinely per-signal/unique (likely needs LLM generation once the local-model backend removes the Groq cost concern — see `feature/002-local-llm-backend`, not yet merged), and use "two signals would get an identical implication" as a signal to consider *consolidating* those two signals rather than listing both — a density/quality argument, not just a text-variety one.
- **NEW (2026-07-13): Malaysia's BER/GENERAL signal density is much lower than Vietnam's — confirmed as a source-quality issue, not a code bug.** MY/BER produced only 1 signal from 10 filter-passed sources (vs. VN/BER's 13 from 8, VN/GENERAL's 41 from 15).
